Recipes by machine and grinder

Casabrews CM5418 / 5418 PRO: your first espresso shot

Start with 14 g of coffee in and 28 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast. The grind number depends on your grinder: find yours below, then taste and adjust.

The recipe

  • Dose: 14 g (the 5418 PRO manual gives 13 to 15 g for the 2-cup filter; the CM5418 manual gives no figure).
  • Stop at: 28 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast. The 1 CUP / 2 CUP buttons stop by a programmed volume. On the 5418 PRO the same button also stops the shot (its manual calls them start/stop): press it when the scale under the cup reads your target weight. On the CM5418, use its volume programming as a stop button: hold 1 CUP for 3 seconds, press 1 CUP to start, and press it again when the scale reads your target. That stops this shot and saves the amount for next time. Use 1 CUP even for a double: the manual sets the 2 CUP amount at 80 ml or more, more water than a double shot needs (Casabrews manual, "Programming the volume").
  • Temperature: No temperature control. It preheats by itself at power-on: wait until the lights stop flashing. On the CM5418, after steaming run hot water from the wand for about 8 s to cool the boiler: until you do, its overheat protection blocks brewing (the 1 CUP / 2 CUP lights flash).

Your grinder is better than your basket. Stock baskets on this machine are pressurised, so grind changes do little; a 51 mm single-wall basket is the upgrade that unlocks it.
