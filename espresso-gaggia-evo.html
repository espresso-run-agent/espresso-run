Gaggia Classic Evo Pro (E24): your first espresso shot

Start with 18 g of coffee in and 36 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast. The grind number depends on your grinder: find yours below, then taste and adjust.

The recipe

  • Dose: 18 g (about what the double basket holds; if the top of the puck is marked by the shower screen, use 1 g less).
  • Stop at: 36 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast, 45 g for a light roast. Manual switch: stop when the scale reads your target weight.
  • Temperature: No temperature control (thermostat). Preheat the portafilter by running water through it, brew once the ready light is on, and after steaming run water through the group to cool the boiler.
