Cafelat Robot: your first espresso shot

Start with 16 g of coffee in and 32 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast. The grind number depends on your grinder: find yours below, then taste and adjust.

The recipe

  • Dose: 16 g (Cafelat's example: 16 g in, about 32 g out).
  • Stop at: 32 g in the cup (1:2) for a medium roast, 40 g for a light roast. Stop by weight.
  • Temperature: Unheated: water just off the boil into a gently preheated basket (Cafelat).
  • Pre-infusion: Ramp the arms gently to start, then hold 6 to 7 bar; Cafelat recommends never more than 8.
