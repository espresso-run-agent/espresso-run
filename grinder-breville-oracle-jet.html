Breville Oracle Jet: espresso grind setting

Start at grind size 22 for a medium roast. Finer is a lower number (dial anticlockwise).

Where to start

  • Light roast: grind size 20. Medium: grind size 22. Dark: grind size 24.
  • The dial: 1 to 45 (grind size).
  • One small step: 1 grind size.
  • Breville's start is the factory setting, 22; change it in small steps and re-dial when you open new beans.

The light and dark starting points are two small steps either side of the medium one: our rule of thumb, not the maker's.
