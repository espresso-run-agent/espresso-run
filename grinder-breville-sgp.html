Breville Smart Grinder Pro: espresso grind setting

Start at setting 12 for a medium roast. Finer is a lower number.

Where to start

  • Light roast: setting 10. Medium: setting 12. Dark: setting 14.
  • Usual espresso range: 1 to 30 (setting).
  • One small step: 1 setting.
  • Out of the box the upper burr sits at 6. If the finest settings still aren't fine enough for an unpressurised basket, move the upper burr finer, one number at a time.

The light and dark starting points are two small steps either side of the medium one: our rule of thumb, not the maker's.
