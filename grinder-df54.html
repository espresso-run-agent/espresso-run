Turin DF54: espresso grind setting

From true zero, turn back to about 15 on the scale (Coffeeness's review), then dial in from there. Finer is a lower number.

Where to start

  • One small step: 1 dial mark.
  • Zero point: Find true zero first (burrs just chirp, grinder empty and running): it is often a few numbers below the printed 0.
  • Record your own numbers: other owners' charts only match if their zero matches yours.
