Turin DF83 (any generation): espresso grind setting

Start a little coarser than where the burrs touch, and dial in from there. Finer is a lower number (owners’ practice; Turin doesn’t say).

Where to start

  • One small step: 1 dial mark.
  • Zero point: Owners find true zero this way (Turin publishes no procedure): grinder empty and running, turn finer until the burrs just chirp. Owners report the zero can shift after taking the burrs out.
  • Turin publishes no espresso number; the stock burrs are espresso-focused. Record your own settings from your true zero.
