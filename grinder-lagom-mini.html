Option-O Lagom Mini (original, not Mini 2): espresso grind setting

Option-O's espresso start: 8 big dots from zero (0.8 of a turn) with Obsidian burrs, or 5 (0.5 of a turn) with Moonshine burrs, which later became the standard. Start there for your burrs and dial in. Finer is the hopper clockwise.

Where to start

  • One small step: 0.5 big dots from zero.
  • Zero point: Zero is the lock point: motor off, turn the hopper clockwise until the burrs lock (Option-O).
  • Option-O advises against very light roasts for espresso until new burrs have bedded in.
  • There are 10 big dots per turn of the hopper.
