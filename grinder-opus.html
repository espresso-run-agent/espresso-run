Fellow Opus (original): espresso grind setting

Start a little coarser than where the burrs touch, and dial in from there. Finer is a lower number, then the inner ring for fine steps.

Where to start

  • One small step: 0.25 setting.
  • Espresso sits at the fine end of the outer ring (41 settings, 1 to 11); use the inner ring for the fine steps between them (Fellow).
