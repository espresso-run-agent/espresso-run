Mazzer Philos: espresso grind setting

Mazzer leaves a gap at 0, so start a few notches above 0 and dial in from there. Finer is a lower number (toward 0).

Where to start

  • One small step: 1 notch, about [object Object] µm of burr movement.
  • Zero point: Mazzer calibrates 0 with a gap left between the burrs. Recalibrate only after changing the burrs, or after taking them out and refitting them.
  • Mazzer publishes no espresso number. The I189D burrs are Mazzer's pick for traditional espresso (medium to dark roasts); the I200D for lighter roasts.
  • It can be converted to stepless for finer control.
