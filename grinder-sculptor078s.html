Timemore Sculptor 078S: espresso grind setting

Start at dial mark 1 for a medium roast. Finer is a lower number.

Where to start

  • Light roast: dial mark 0.8. Medium: dial mark 1. Dark: dial mark 1.2.
  • Usual espresso range: 0.6 to 1.6 (dial mark).
  • One small step: 0.1 dial mark.
  • The espresso band is narrow (about 0.6 to 1.6); one tenth of a mark moves shot time by a few seconds.

The light and dark starting points are two small steps either side of the medium one: our rule of thumb, not the maker's.
