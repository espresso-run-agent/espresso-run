Baratza Sette 270 / 270Wi: espresso grind setting

Start at macro setting 6 for a medium roast. Finer is a lower macro number, or the micro ring toward A.

Where to start

  • Light roast: macro setting 4. Medium: macro setting 6. Dark: macro setting 8.
  • Usual espresso range: 3 to 9 (macro setting).
  • One small step: 1 macro setting.
  • Baratza's espresso range is macro 3 to 9 with the micro ring at E; use the micro ring (A fine, I coarse) for the small steps.
  • Adjust with the motor running.

The light and dark starting points are two small steps either side of the medium one: our rule of thumb, not the maker's.
