How it works

  • The basket sets the dose. Stay within a gram or two of what your basket is made for.
  • The roast sets the ratio and temperature. About 1:2.5 and hot for light roasts, 1:2 for medium, shorter and cooler for dark.
  • Then the grind gets the shot into range, and the yield fine-tunes it. Temperature comes last. Change one thing at a time, and stop the shot by weight, not by time.
  • Sour and bitter at once usually means uneven extraction, so the fix is puck prep and a slightly coarser grind, not a finer one.
  • This is the method that James Hoffmann, Lance Hedrick, Matt Perger and Scott Rao teach, applied to the settings your machine and grinder have. It is a starting point: grinders differ from unit to unit, and your taste is the judge.
